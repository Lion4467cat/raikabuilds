02.2 · Capstone Major Project BMSCE

NutriSense — Autonomous 3D Dietary Metrology & Statutory Audit

GitHub ↗
  • 3D Dietary Metrology: Architected an 8-subsystem single-photo pipeline for PM POSHAN mid-day meals; used Google SigLIP-2 (α = 0.65 background attenuation) to classify 115 Indian dishes and Microsoft MoGe-2 metric depth with SVD plane fitting (3.5 mm plate tare) to reconstruct food volume.
